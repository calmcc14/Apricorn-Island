## Background prompt

Create an original game background asset, landscape 3:2 aspect ratio. Pixel art, crisp visibly square pixels, limited 24-color palette, retro RPG aesthetic reminiscent of Pokemon overworld scenery and Deltarune's clean chunky pixel shapes. Soft lavender sky fading by discrete flat color bands into pale buttery cream and light mint ground. At the far background only: spaced rounded tall pastel cyan and yellow-green hills with sparse blocky darker patches, a few tiny rectangular clouds. Along far left and right edges a few small dark teal layered pixel pine trees and two pink flower clusters, a tiny red-roof healing hut in upper right far distance. The middle 75 percent and lower half must be very open, pale, quiet and uncluttered for a route UI overlay. No paths, no roads, no level pads, no circles, no markers, no characters, no text, no UI. This is an actual background to use in a game, not a screenshot or mockup. Avoid gradients, blur, painterly effects, outlines around every scenery object. Warm whimsical retro adventure mood.

## Trainer prompt

Create one original friendly adult explorer character for a retro pixel-art video game. Full body standing pose, front view. Red baseball cap, teal jacket, cream shirt, navy trousers, brown boots, small yellow backpack. Large head and compact pixel-RPG proportions. Crisp square pixel edges and a limited color palette, dark outlines. Single centered sprite on a transparent background. No text, no scene, no frame, no platform, no other characters. Approximately 24 by 36 pixels of detail, enlarged with hard square pixel edges.
