# Pokémon sprites

The National Pokédex includes front and back sprites for all 1,025 species.
The existing sprites and alternate forms come from PokéAPI's `front_default`
and `back_default` collection. The remaining species were added from that same
collection at revision `c0542f7c51215d9672439012838c99f520596cbd`.
The front and back shiny sprites for all 1,025 species and the six alternate
forms use the same revision's `shiny/` and `back/shiny/` collections.

Source repository: https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites
Sprite artist and community credits: https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites#special-thanks

The game stores sprites locally for offline play.
Pokémon and Pokémon character images belong to their respective owners,
including Nintendo, Creatures, and GAME FREAK. This is an unofficial private fan project.
