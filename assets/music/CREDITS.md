Original battle music composed for Apricorn Island, inspired by handheld monster-battling RPGs. No Pokémon recordings or melodies are included.

- Wild: bright pulse lead, triangle bass, arpeggios and noise percussion.
- Rival: faster, lively battle theme.
- Guardian: tense minor-key boss theme.

Each track loops for 32 bars. Run compose.py to regenerate the WAV files.

The Glitch Dimension's ambient track uses the supplied `Board_ocean_music.ogg`. The ambient fallback, interference layer, and battle theme were composed for Apricorn Island. All boot, modem, crash, and static sounds are synthesized. Run `compose_glitch.py` to regenerate the original loops.
